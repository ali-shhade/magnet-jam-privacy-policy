1. Information stored by Magnet Jam

The App stores gameplay information on your device so that it can provide the game and preserve your progress. This may include level progress, game settings, hint availability, and records needed to safely restore rewarded-hint transactions. These game records are stored locally in the App's private storage; the App's own gameplay system does not upload them to a Magnet Jam account or developer-operated game server.

Local game data generally remains on your device until you clear the App's storage, reset the game data using an available in-game option, or uninstall the App. Clearing local data or uninstalling the App may permanently remove locally saved progress.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (Next-Gen SDK) to provide optional rewarded video advertisements. When this SDK operates, Google may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video-ad views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, stability, and resource usage; and
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with an account on the device.

Google uses this information for purposes that may include delivering and measuring ads, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Depending on your location, available privacy choices, and applicable settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). For more information about Google's data practices, please read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK.

3. Consent and privacy choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to present privacy and consent messages when required and to manage the choices made through those messages. The choices available to you can depend on your region and applicable privacy requirements. Where the App provides a privacy-options entry point, you can use it to review or change the choices available to you.

You can also review advertising-related controls in your Android device settings. Changing device settings may affect advertising identifiers or ad personalization, but it does not necessarily erase information already processed by Google.

4. How information is shared

Magnet Jam does not sell gameplay records stored locally on your device. Information collected through the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted to Google and may be processed or shared by Google as described in Google's privacy documentation and applicable advertising settings. The App does not operate its own advertising-data brokerage service.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data is retained on your device until it is cleared, reset, or removed by uninstalling the App. Google controls the retention of information collected through its advertising services. Magnet Jam does not control Google's retention periods; please consult Google's privacy policy and privacy controls for details about Google's handling of that information.

6. Children's privacy

The App's intended age groups and advertising configuration are described in its Google Play listing and are subject to the age-related requirements that apply in each market. The App does not ask players to submit their name, email address, phone number, or other direct contact details to play. Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern may contact the developer using the contact details above.

7. Security

Gameplay records are stored in the App's private storage on the device. Google describes encryption in transit for information collected by the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. International processing

Google and its service providers may process advertising-related information in countries where they or their service providers operate. Such processing is subject to Google's policies and the safeguards applicable to the relevant service.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published at the same URL with an updated effective date.
